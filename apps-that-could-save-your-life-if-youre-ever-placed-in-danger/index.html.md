# 7 Personal Safety Apps That Could Save Your Life in an Emergency

There are essential safety apps that could save your life, which every woman should consider installing on her smartphone in case of an unexpected emergency or dangerous situation. If you ever find yourself walking home alone at night or navigating unfamiliar surroundings, taking proactive steps is vital. Because emergency situations can happen when least expected, playing it safe with a reliable personal danger alert app ensures help is always just a tap away.

When selecting a mobile safety tool, look for key functionality that fits your daily routine:

- Instant GPS location sharing with emergency contacts

- One-tap SOS triggers and silent panic buttons

- Automated check-in timers for solo commutes

## 1. My Force

_ **On iTunes at:** itunes.apple.com_

All you need to do to stay safe is press a single button on your phone. If you feel you are in a dangerous situation or suspect you are being followed, the app sends an immediate alert to a live monitoring team, allowing them to track your exact location and record ambient audio. If security agents verify you are in danger, they swiftly dispatch the appropriate local emergency response team. This emergency service makes protecting yourself effortless, even if you prefer notifying a custom [circle of trusted contacts](#2) for immediate backup.

## 2. Circle of Six

![Circle of Six](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/g2/qb/cri5wa8p_320x568.jpg)

**On iTunes at:** [itunes.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/circle-of-6/id509293952)

This award-winning app helps your friends and family members keep tabs on your whereabouts. You select six trusted contacts to join your personal safety network. If you are ever stranded, feeling unsafe, or need a quick ride home, sending a pre-programmed alert automatically broadcasts your precise GPS coordinates to your group. The app also comes pre-loaded with direct lines to national safety hotlines for immediate support whenever needed.

## 3. On Watch

![On Watch](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mr/a9/mb34fm0b_320x568.jpg)

**On iTunes at:** [itunes.apple.com](https://web.archive.org/web/https://apps.apple.com/us/app/onwatch-personal-safety-app/id473517240?amp%3Bat=11l4BD)

Tailored specifically for college students and solo travelers, On Watch allows you to contact campus police or local authorities in seconds. You can configure customized emergency messages to dispatch to close friends if you fail to check in after an evening class. This app ensures you never have to navigate late-night campus walks without a fast connection to safety personnel.

## 4. Watch over Me

_ **On iTunes at:** itunes.apple.com_

Whenever you are walking through an unfamiliar area or a neighborhood with elevated crime risk, Watch over Me acts as your personal digital escort. Set a timer for how long your journey should take; if you fail to check in safely before the time expires, the app instantly alerts your designated emergency contacts with your last known location. It also functions as a proactive neighborhood alert system to keep you informed of safety conditions around you.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Circle of Six | Peer network alert | Night out with friends |
| Watch over Me | Timer-based check-in | Solo walks & commutes |

## 5. BSafe

![BSafe](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/sa/f6/ah7m1pyy_320x568.jpg)

**On iTunes at:** [itunes.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bsafe-personal-safety-app/id459709106?amp%3Bat=11l4BD)

BSafe provides versatile personal protection features, including realistic fake phone calls to help you politely exit uncomfortable or awkward conversations. If an actual threat arises, tapping the SOS button immediately alerts your emergency contact group, streams live video, and triggers an audible alarm siren to deter attackers and draw immediate attention. Pairing this with a [location tracking fail-safe](#4) gives you comprehensive peace of mind.

## 6. Hollaback

_ **On iTunes at:** itunes.apple.com_

Designed to combat street harassment and catcalling, Hollaback empowers users to report incidents directly onto an interactive community map. By logging the location, details, and optional photos of harassment, you help build community awareness and combat public safety threats. It is a powerful tool for women taking an active stance against street-level harassment.

## 7. Cab4Me

![Cab4Me](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ve/w1/ehejoipv_320x568.jpg)

**On iTunes at:** [itunes.apple.com](https://goo.gl/NicCHX)

Cab4Me uses your device location to generate an instant list of reliable local taxi services, complete with phone numbers and estimated fare rates. Having quick access to verified transportation ensures you can secure a safe ride home whenever public transport or walking alone feels unsafe. Combine this with your [siren button features](#5) for complete security while traveling late at night.

If a simple mobile download has the potential to keep you safe in a crisis, keeping at least one danger alert app active on your device is a smart precaution. Stay vigilant and take full advantage of modern safety technology. Do you have a favorite personal safety app that gives you extra peace of mind when traveling alone?
